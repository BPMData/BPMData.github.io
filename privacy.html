Privacy policy

AllAboard (allaboard.ai.studio), plus OverBoard, OverHeard and BibleViz. Effective November 15, 2026.

AllAboard is a free, shared classroom whiteboard. Students join a room with a room code and a display name; there are no accounts. AllAboard has no ads and no analytics or tracking scripts, and no data is sold or shared for marketing. In Google's sign-in screens, AllAboard appears as AllAboard & OverBoard, the name these apps share.

1. What AllAboard stores

A room is shared, so what people put in it is stored on AllAboard's server so everyone in the room can see it.

2. Google user data (Google Drive)

AllAboard works fully without a Google account. Google Drive is an optional feature for the teacher of a room; students never see these buttons.

What AllAboard can access

AllAboard asks Google for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file ("See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app"). With it, AllAboard can access only:

  • the files you choose yourself in Google's file picker, and
  • the room snapshot files AllAboard saves to your Drive at your request.

AllAboard cannot see, list or search the rest of your Drive. It does not request your name, email address, profile picture, contacts or any other Google account information.

How AllAboard uses it

ButtonWhat happens
From DriveOpens Google's file picker, showing only Google Slides and PDF files. AllAboard downloads the file you pick (Google converts Slides to PDF), turns the pages you choose into images in your browser, and adds them to your room as a worksheet.
Save to DriveSaves a snapshot of your room to your Drive as a .json file: its drawings, text, chat, display names, worksheets and breakout boards. Saving the same room again updates that file instead of making a new one.
Open from DriveOpens Google's file picker, showing only .json files. AllAboard reads the snapshot you pick and shows a preview; nothing changes in your room until you confirm.

Google Drive data is used only for these features, only when you click them.

Where it goes and how long it's kept

  • Files move directly between your browser and Google. Your Drive files and Google sign-in are never sent to or stored on AllAboard's server.
  • The exception is content you deliberately add to a room: worksheet pages from From Drive, or a snapshot you apply with Open from Drive. That becomes room content, visible to the room and deleted with it, as in section 1.
  • Google's access token is kept in your browser's memory only. It expires within about an hour and is gone when you reload or close the page. AllAboard never saves it.
  • The only Drive information kept in your browser is the ID of each room's last saved snapshot, so the next save updates the same file.
  • Snapshots saved to your Drive are yours. They stay in your Drive until you delete them there.

Sharing

AllAboard does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models. No person reads it. The only people who see Drive content are the people in the room you choose to add it to.

Removing access

You can remove AllAboard's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (look for "AllAboard & OverBoard"). To remove Drive content from a room, clear the room or let it expire after 7 days.

Limited Use

AllAboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Students and schools

AllAboard doesn't ask students for an email address, account or real name; a display name can be anything. Because rooms are shared, students should not put personal information in names, drawings or chat. Teachers should follow their school's rules for classroom technology. School Google accounts may need an administrator to allow AllAboard before the Drive buttons work. If personal information about a child is in a room, the teacher can clear the room, or contact us and we will delete it.

4. Security

AllAboard is served only over HTTPS. Teacher actions in a room require the room's teacher PIN.

5. OverBoard, OverHeard and BibleViz

OverBoard (bpmdata.github.io/OverBoard) runs entirely in your browser. Your PDFs, worksheets, ink and settings stay on your computer; nothing is uploaded. If you choose to send a Board not working? report, it includes the board brand and model you enter, the problems you tick, what the board reported during the touch test, and technical details about the browser and computer (such as browser and operating system version, screen size, and language settings). Those details are posted as a public issue on OverBoard's GitHub page. Any comments and email address you add are never made public: they are emailed privately to the OverBoard reports inbox, and the email address is used only for a thank-you and one follow-up when there is a fix to try.

OverHeard (over-heard.ai.studio) turns speech into captions and translations. It needs microphone permission, and it works like this:

  • Speech to text uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. In Chrome and Edge, the browser may send the audio to Google or Microsoft to recognize it, under their terms. OverHeard never receives or records audio.
  • Translation: the recognized text is sent to OverHeard's server, which translates it with Google Translate or Google's Gemini API, under Google's terms. If you pick the on-device translator in Chrome, translation happens in your browser instead.
  • Not stored: OverHeard doesn't save transcripts or translations. The server keeps recent translations briefly in memory to answer repeated phrases faster, and they are gone when it restarts. Its logs record only counts, such as how many translations were made, never the text or your IP address.
  • In your browser: your settings (languages, the names used in Conversation mode, and the caption window layout) are saved on your device, plus a random ID for the current tab, used to keep one person from overloading the translators.

Because speech can include anything said in the room, avoid saying sensitive personal information while OverHeard is listening.

BibleViz runs in your browser and collects nothing.
