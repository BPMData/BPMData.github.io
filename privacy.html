Privacy policy

Covers AllAboard, OverBoard and BibleViz. Effective November 15, 2026.

These apps are free classroom tools. They have no ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. This page explains exactly what each app keeps.

AllAboard

AllAboard is a shared whiteboard, so the things people put in a room are stored on our server so that everyone in the room can see them.

WhatWhyWhere and how long
The display name you type (it doesn't have to be a real name)To show who drew or wrote whatOn AllAboard's server (Google Cloud, United States). A room and everything in it is deleted automatically after 7 days with no activity. A teacher can clear a room at any time.
Drawings, text, stamps and chat messages in a roomTo share them with the room
Worksheets a teacher uploads (images or PDF pages)To show them on the board
The teacher PIN for a roomTo let the teacher manage the room
A random ID, your name, and the room's teacher PIN, saved in your browserSo you rejoin as the same person after a reloadOnly in your browser, until you clear site data

Like any website, the hosting service (Google Cloud) keeps standard request logs, such as IP address and time of request, for operating and securing the service.

A teacher can save a room as a .json or image file. That file goes to the teacher's own device (or Google Drive, below), not to us.

Google Drive and Sign in with Google (optional)

AllAboard works without a Google account. If a teacher chooses to use the Google Drive buttons:

  • AllAboard asks for the drive.file permission only. It can open only the files you pick in Google's file picker, and the room snapshots AllAboard saves for you. It cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • Files are opened and saved directly between your browser and Google. Google Drive data never passes through or is stored on AllAboard's server. The exception is a worksheet you import: its pages are added to your room just like an uploaded worksheet, as described above.
  • Google's sign-in token is kept in your browser's memory only, and is gone when you close or reload the page. AllAboard never stores it.
  • If you use Sign in with Google on the join screen, AllAboard reads your first name only to fill in the name box. Nothing else from your account is used or stored.
  • You can remove AllAboard's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

AllAboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features you choose, is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not read by people.

OverBoard

OverBoard runs entirely in your browser. The PDFs, pictures and worksheets you open, your ink, and your settings stay on your computer, in the browser's storage or in files you choose to save. Nothing is uploaded.

"Board not working?" reports (optional)

If you choose to send a report, it includes the board brand and model you enter, the problems you tick, what the board reported during the touch test, and technical details about the browser and computer (such as browser and operating system version, screen size, and language settings). You may also add comments and an email address.

  • The board, computer and test details are posted as a public issue on OverBoard's GitHub page so the problem can be fixed.
  • Your comments and email address are never made public. They are emailed privately to the OverBoard reports inbox.
  • If you give an email address, it is used only to send you a thank-you and one follow-up when there is a fix to try. Ask us to delete it at any time.

BibleViz

BibleViz runs in your browser and collects nothing. Your searches are not sent anywhere.

Students and schools

These apps don't ask students for an email address, account or real name. A display name can be anything. Because AllAboard rooms are shared, students should not put personal information in names, drawings or chat. Teachers should follow their school's rules on classroom technology. If you believe a child's personal information is in an AllAboard room, a teacher can clear the room, or contact us and we will delete it.
