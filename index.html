OverHeard

UN-style simultaneous interpretation

Live captions and translation for multilingual learners. Put what you say on the projector in English and up to three other languages, or hold a two-way translated conversation.

Open OverHeard
  • Open a lecture room with a PIN. Students go to its page on their own devices, plug in headphones and hear what you're saying, moments after you say it, in their language spoken by a natural-sounding voice.
  • 12 languages, including Spanish, Arabic, Haitian Creole, Somali, Dari and Pashto.
  • Captions pop out and float over your slides, PDFs or any other window.

OverBoard

Built for teaching all day

Smartboard ink that sticks to the page. Write on a PDF or worksheet, scroll, and your ink scrolls with it.

Open OverBoard
  • Separate ink for each class period on the same worksheet, so 3rd period's notes are still there when 3rd period comes back.
  • A big countdown timer with a chime, palm eraser, and covers that hide answers until you wipe them away.
  • Opens whole textbooks, exports PDFs with your ink, and works offline once installed.

AllAboard

Real-time multiplayer classroom whiteboard

Students join with a room code and draw, write and chat together, with worksheets, breakout boards and live teacher controls.

Open AllAboard
  • Per-student isolated ink layers: students erase their own work without touching a classmate's drawing or the teacher's worksheet.
  • Breakout rooms with auto-randomize, manual drag-and-drop, or student self-select — up to 8 groups with a live teacher grid overview.
  • Drop in PDF or image worksheets as a locked background, or use built-in templates like 4-Box Frayer Models and task check-in columns.

AllHail

AI vision and speech for blind and low-vision students

Point the camera at a worksheet, painting or document and tap one button. AllHail speaks a full description, even offline.

Open AllHail
  • One-tap camera capture with Quick look and Full detail modes: from a short overview to every handwritten answer, grade and visual detail.
  • On-device speech chain: browser voices, Kokoro and Piper run locally; cloud voices are a fallback, not a requirement.
  • Teachers upload documents, edit the description, preview and download an MP3 to post on Google Classroom.
