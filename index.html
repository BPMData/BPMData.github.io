Search tips
  • Capitalization doesn't matter, but spelling does. This is the King James Version, so it uses older British spellings (neighbour, not neighbor).
  • Peter also finds Simon. Satan also finds the devil and tempter.
  • If you search a longer phrase and a shorter one inside it (for example Mary Magdalene and Mary), mentions of the longer phrase aren't counted again under the shorter one.
  • Mark also finds the ordinary word "mark" (see Revelation).
  • The address bar updates with your search, so you can copy the link to share it.
