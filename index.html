Classroom apps

Free apps for teaching. They run in the browser, with no accounts and no ads.

OverHeard

Live captions and translation for multilingual learners. Put what you say on the projector in English and students' languages, or hold a two-way translated conversation.

Open OverHeard →

OverBoard

Smartboard ink that sticks to the page. Write on a PDF or worksheet, scroll, and your ink scrolls with it. Palm eraser, highlighter and PDF export.

Open OverBoard →

AllAboard

A shared whiteboard for the whole class. Students join with a room code and draw, write and chat together, with worksheets, breakout boards and teacher controls.

Open AllAboard →

AllHail

Photos and documents read aloud for blind and low-vision students. Take or pick a picture for a quick overview or full description, with offline browser voices.
